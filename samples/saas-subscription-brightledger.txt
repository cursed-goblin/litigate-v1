BRIGHTLEDGER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Subscription Agreement is made on 22 February 2026 between Brightledger
Software Inc, a Delaware corporation, the provider, and Meridian Systems Private
Limited, the customer. The provider supplies a hosted financial close and
reconciliation platform on a subscription basis for two hundred fifty named
users.

1. Subscription and Access
The provider grants the customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to
access the platform during the subscription term for its internal business
purposes. The customer shall not permit access by any third party, shall not
resell access, and remains responsible for all activity under its accounts.

2. Subscription Term and Renewal
The initial term is twenty-four months from the activation date. The
subscription renews automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless
either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty days before the
end of the then-current term. Notice given late takes effect at the following
renewal.

3. Fees and Price Increases
The annual subscription fee is one hundred forty-four thousand dollars, invoiced
annually in advance. On each renewal the provider may increase the fee by up to
seven percent above the prior year's fee without the customer's consent, and by
more than seven percent only with the customer's written agreement.

4. Payment
Invoices are payable within forty-five days. The provider may suspend access if
any undisputed invoice remains unpaid for more than fifteen days after written
reminder. Fees are non-refundable and no credit is given for unused capacity,
inactive users, or periods of voluntary non-use.

5. Service Availability
The provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the platform
available ninety-nine and one half percent of the time each month, excluding
scheduled maintenance and events outside its reasonable control. The provider
does not offer service credits, and availability shortfalls are not treated as a
breach of this Agreement.

6. Support
The provider will respond to critical incidents within four business hours and
to all other requests within two business days, during nine to six Eastern time
on business days. Support is provided in English only and does not include
configuration work, data cleansing, or custom report building.

7. Customer Data
The customer retains all right, title, and interest in data it uploads. The
provider may use aggregated and de-identified usage data to operate, secure, and
improve its products, and to produce industry benchmarks, provided that no
output identifies the customer or any individual.

8. Data Location and Security
Customer data is stored in the provider's United States and Ireland regions. The
provider maintains an information security programme aligned to recognised
industry standards, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and will make its most
recent third-party audit report available on request once per year.

9. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret, use it only
to perform this Agreement, and protect it with reasonable care. These
obligations continue for five years after termination, and indefinitely in
respect of source code and security documentation.

10. Warranty and Disclaimer
The provider warrants that the platform will perform materially in accordance
with its published documentation. Except as expressly stated, the platform is
provided as is and the provider disclaims all other warranties, including any
implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

11. Indemnity
The provider will defend the customer against third-party claims that the
platform infringes a patent or copyright, provided the customer notifies it
promptly and allows it to control the defence. The customer will defend the
provider against claims arising from the customer's data or its use of the
platform in breach of this Agreement.

12. Limitation of Liability
Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential
loss, or for lost profits, revenue, or data. Each party's total liability is
capped at the fees paid by the customer in the twelve months before the event
giving rise to the claim.

13. Suspension and Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured thirty days
after written notice. The provider may suspend access immediately where it
reasonably believes continued access presents a security risk to the platform or
to other customers, and will restore access once the risk is resolved.

14. Data Export on Exit
For thirty days after termination the customer may export its data in the
provider's standard comma-separated format. After that period the provider will
delete customer data within sixty days. Migration assistance beyond standard
export is chargeable at the provider's professional services rates.

15. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and the parties
submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in
New York County. Each party waives any objection to that venue on grounds of
inconvenience.
