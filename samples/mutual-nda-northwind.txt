MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into on 12 March 2026 between
Northwind Analytics Limited, a company registered in Bengaluru, India, and
Meridian Systems Private Limited, a company registered in Chennai, India. Each
party may disclose confidential information to the other in connection with
evaluating a possible commercial relationship in data infrastructure services.

1. Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information means any non-public information disclosed by one party
to the other, whether in writing, orally, or by inspection of tangible objects,
that is designated as confidential at the time of disclosure or that a
reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the
information and the circumstances of disclosure.

2. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes
publicly available through no fault of the receiving party, was rightfully in
the receiving party's possession before disclosure, is independently developed
by the receiving party without reference to the disclosing party's information,
or is rightfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence.

3. Obligations of the Receiving Party
The receiving party shall use the Confidential Information solely to evaluate
the potential relationship described above, shall protect it using at least the
same degree of care it applies to its own confidential information of similar
importance, and shall not disclose it to any third party without the prior
written consent of the disclosing party.

4. Permitted Disclosures
The receiving party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees,
officers, and professional advisers who have a genuine need to know it for the
permitted purpose, provided that each such recipient is bound by written or
professional obligations of confidentiality that are no less protective than
those set out in this Agreement.

5. Compelled Disclosure
If the receiving party is required by law, regulation, or court order to
disclose any Confidential Information, it shall, where legally permitted, give
the disclosing party prompt written notice so that the disclosing party may seek
a protective order, and shall disclose only the minimum portion of the
information that is legally required.

6. Term and Survival
This Agreement takes effect on the date above and continues for two years.
The confidentiality obligations in clauses 3 and 4 survive for three years from
the date of each disclosure, after which the receiving party's obligations with
respect to that disclosure expire automatically without further notice.

7. Return or Destruction of Materials
On written request by the disclosing party, the receiving party shall promptly
return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information and confirm
in writing that it has done so, except that one archival copy may be retained
solely to evidence compliance with legal or record-keeping obligations.

8. No Licence and No Warranty
Nothing in this Agreement grants either party any licence, right, title, or
interest in the other party's intellectual property. All Confidential
Information is provided as is, and neither party makes any representation or
warranty as to its accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any purpose.

9. No Obligation to Proceed
Neither party is under any obligation to enter into any further agreement, to
purchase or supply any goods or services, or to continue discussions. Each party
may terminate discussions at any time for any reason, and neither party is
liable to the other for costs incurred during the evaluation.

10. Remedies
The parties agree that unauthorised disclosure of Confidential Information may
cause harm for which money alone is an inadequate answer, and that the affected
party may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other rights available to it
at law, without needing to post a bond or other security.

11. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Chennai have
exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it.
The parties will attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through discussion
between senior representatives before commencing proceedings.

12. Entire Agreement
This Agreement is the entire understanding between the parties concerning the
exchange of Confidential Information and supersedes all prior discussions on
that subject. It may be amended only by a written document signed by an
authorised representative of each party.
