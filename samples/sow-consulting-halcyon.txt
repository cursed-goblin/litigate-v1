STATEMENT OF WORK NUMBER SOW-2026-004

This Statement of Work is issued under the Professional Services Agreement
dated 18 September 2025 between Halcyon Advisory LLP, the consultant, and
Meridian Systems Private Limited, the client. The consultant will deliver a
migration of the client's reporting platform from an on-premise warehouse to a
managed cloud warehouse over an estimated fourteen weeks.

1. Scope of Services
The consultant shall carry out discovery and current-state assessment, target
architecture design, migration of up to two hundred reporting objects,
validation of migrated outputs against existing reports, and two workshops to
hand over operational knowledge to the client's data team.

2. Deliverables
The consultant will provide an assessment report, a target architecture
document, a migration runbook, a validation summary comparing old and new report
outputs, and recorded handover sessions. All deliverables are provided in
electronic form in the consultant's standard templates.

3. Timeline and Dependencies
The estimated schedule assumes the client provides production-equivalent test
data within five business days of kick-off and nominates a single decision maker
for sign-off. Any delay in a client dependency extends the schedule by at least
the duration of the delay and may attract standby charges.

4. Fees
The fees for this Statement of Work are eighty-two thousand United States
dollars, invoiced monthly in arrears in proportion to work performed. Fees are
exclusive of applicable taxes, which are payable by the client in addition at
the prevailing rate.

5. Payment Terms
Invoices are payable within ninety days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts
attract interest at one and one half percent per month, compounded monthly, and
the consultant may suspend all work on seven days written notice if any invoice
remains unpaid for more than thirty days after its due date.

6. Expenses
The client shall reimburse reasonable travel, accommodation, and subsistence
expenses at cost plus a ten percent administration charge. Air travel of more
than four hours may be booked in business class. Expenses do not require prior
approval where the individual item is below one thousand dollars.

7. Change Control
Any change to scope, deliverables, or timeline requires a written change order
signed by both parties. Additional work is charged at the consultant's
then-current standard rates, which the consultant may revise once per calendar
year on thirty days notice to the client.

8. Acceptance
The client shall review each deliverable and provide written acceptance or a
reasonably detailed statement of deficiencies within five business days of
receipt. If the client does not respond within that period the deliverable is
deemed accepted and the associated payment milestone becomes due.

9. Personnel
The consultant will assign a named engagement lead and up to three
consultants. The consultant may substitute personnel of equivalent skill and
experience at its discretion and will inform the client of any change to the
named engagement lead within five business days.

10. Intellectual Property
The client owns the deliverables on full payment of all fees due under this
Statement of Work. The consultant retains ownership of its pre-existing
methodologies, tools, templates, and know-how, and grants the client a
non-exclusive licence to use them as embedded in the deliverables.

11. Warranty
The consultant warrants that the services will be performed with reasonable
skill and care by suitably qualified personnel. The consultant's sole obligation
for breach of this warranty is to re-perform the affected services, provided the
client reports the defect within thirty days of delivery.

12. Termination
Either party may terminate this Statement of Work for convenience on thirty
days written notice. On termination the client shall pay for all services
performed and expenses incurred up to the termination date, together with any
non-cancellable commitments the consultant has properly entered into.

13. Non-Solicitation
During the term and for twelve months afterwards, neither party shall solicit
for employment any personnel of the other who were directly involved in this
engagement, except through general advertising not targeted at those
individuals.

14. Order of Precedence
In the event of conflict, the Professional Services Agreement prevails over
this Statement of Work except as to scope, fees, and timeline, where this
Statement of Work prevails. Nothing in this document varies the limitation of
liability in the Professional Services Agreement.
