MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made on 15 May 2026 between Vertexgrid
Holdings LLC, a Nevada limited liability company, the customer, and Meridian
Systems Private Limited, the supplier. The supplier will provide software
engineering, integration, and managed support services under statements of work
issued from time to time by the customer.

1. Services and Statements of Work
The supplier shall perform the services described in each statement of work.
The customer may issue, amend, or withdraw a statement of work at any time on
written notice, and the supplier shall implement the change without adjustment to
fees unless the customer agrees otherwise in writing.

2. Fees and Rate Review
Fees are as set out in each statement of work and are fixed for the duration of
this Agreement. The supplier shall not increase its rates for any reason,
including inflation, currency movement, statutory wage change, or increase in
its own costs of delivery, for a period of five years.

3. Payment Terms
The customer shall pay correctly rendered invoices within one hundred and
twenty days of the end of the month in which the invoice is received. The
customer may withhold payment of any invoice in whole while any dispute of any
value remains open, without interest accruing on the withheld amount.

4. Termination for Convenience
The customer may terminate this Agreement or any statement of work at any time
for convenience with immediate effect and without payment of any termination
fee, wind-down cost, or compensation for work in progress. The supplier has no
right to terminate for convenience at any time.

5. Intellectual Property Assignment
The supplier assigns to the customer all intellectual property rights in
everything created in connection with the services, together with all of the
supplier's pre-existing tools, libraries, frameworks, and know-how used or
referenced in delivering the services, on a worldwide, perpetual, and
irrevocable basis.

6. Indemnity
The supplier shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the customer, its
affiliates, and their officers, directors, employees, and end customers against
any and all claims, losses, liabilities, damages, penalties, and expenses
including legal fees on a full indemnity basis, however arising and regardless of
fault or negligence of the customer.

7. Limitation of Liability
The customer's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to five
thousand dollars. The supplier's liability is unlimited in all respects, and no
exclusion of indirect, consequential, or punitive damages applies to the
supplier. The supplier waives any right to claim contribution from the customer.

8. Insurance
The supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than
ten million dollars per claim and in the aggregate, commercial general liability
of five million dollars, and cyber liability of ten million dollars, and shall
name the customer as an additional insured on each policy.

9. Service Levels and Credits
The supplier shall maintain a monthly resolution rate of ninety-nine percent for
critical incidents. Failure in any month attracts a credit of fifteen percent of
the monthly fee per failure, cumulative and without cap, and three failures in
any rolling six months constitute material breach.

10. Audit and Access
The customer or its nominated auditor may audit the supplier's books, systems,
facilities, personnel records, and subcontractor arrangements at any time on
two business days notice, at the supplier's cost, including reimbursement of the
auditor's professional fees and travel expenses.

11. Exclusivity and Non-Compete
During the term and for three years afterwards, the supplier shall not provide
services of any kind to any business that competes with the customer in any
territory, and shall not accept any engagement that the customer notifies in
writing as being with a competitor or a prospective competitor.

12. Personnel and Key Persons
The supplier shall not remove or reassign any named key person without the
customer's prior written consent, which may be withheld at the customer's sole
discretion. The customer may require replacement of any supplier personnel at
any time without reason and at the supplier's cost.

13. Confidentiality
The supplier shall keep all information relating to the customer confidential in
perpetuity, with no exception for information that becomes public, that the
supplier independently develops, or that the supplier already held before
disclosure. Disclosure compelled by law requires the customer's prior written
consent.

14. Data Protection
The supplier shall comply with all applicable data protection laws, shall
process personal data only as instructed, and shall be solely responsible for
any regulatory fine, penalty, or claim relating to personal data processed under
this Agreement, irrespective of whether the customer's instructions caused it.

15. Publicity
The supplier shall not refer to the customer, use the customer's name or logo,
or describe the services in any marketing material, case study, credential list,
or investor document at any time, including after termination, without the
customer's prior written consent.

16. Assignment and Change of Control
The customer may assign or novate this Agreement to any person without consent.
The supplier may not assign, subcontract, or novate any part of it, and a change
in control of the supplier entitles the customer to terminate immediately and
reclaim all fees paid in the preceding twelve months.

17. Unilateral Amendment
The customer may amend the terms of this Agreement, including the schedules and
policies referenced in it, by publishing a revised version to the supplier
portal. The revised version takes effect fifteen days after publication and
applies to all statements of work then in progress.

18. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Nevada. Any dispute shall
be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Clark County,
Nevada, and the supplier irrevocably waives any right to a jury trial and any
objection to that forum.
