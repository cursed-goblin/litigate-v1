RESELLER AND CHANNEL PARTNER AGREEMENT

This Reseller Agreement is entered into on 6 April 2026 between Orionpath
Technologies FZ-LLC, the principal, and Meridian Systems Private Limited, the
reseller. The reseller is appointed to market and resell the principal's
industrial monitoring hardware and associated software subscriptions in the
territory of India, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh.

1. Appointment and Exclusivity
The principal appoints the reseller as its exclusive reseller in the territory
for an initial period of three years. In exchange the reseller agrees not to
market, distribute, or promote any product that competes with the principal's
products anywhere in the world during the term of this Agreement.

2. Minimum Purchase Commitment
The reseller commits to purchase products with a net invoice value of not less
than six hundred thousand dollars in year one, nine hundred thousand dollars in
year two, and one million two hundred thousand dollars in year three. Shortfalls
are invoiced in full within thirty days of the anniversary date.

3. Loss of Exclusivity
If the reseller fails to meet any annual minimum commitment, the principal may
convert the appointment to non-exclusive immediately on written notice, appoint
additional resellers in the territory, and sell directly to any customer,
without any obligation to compensate the reseller for accounts it introduced.

4. Pricing and Discount
The reseller purchases at the principal's list price less thirty percent. The
principal may amend its list prices and discount schedule at any time on fifteen
days notice, and the amended prices apply to all orders accepted after the
effective date, including orders already quoted to end customers.

5. Orders and Acceptance
All purchase orders are subject to acceptance by the principal at its
discretion. The principal is not liable for any failure or delay in accepting or
fulfilling an order, and gives no commitment as to delivery dates. Delivery
estimates are indicative only and time is not of the essence.

6. Payment Terms
Payment is due within thirty days of invoice by wire transfer in United States
dollars, without set-off or deduction of any kind. Overdue amounts attract
interest at two percent per month. The principal may require prepayment or a
letter of credit at any time at its discretion.

7. Marketing Commitment
The reseller shall spend not less than five percent of its annual net revenue
from the products on marketing in the territory, maintain at least four
certified sales staff and two certified engineers, and attend the principal's
annual partner conference at the reseller's own cost.

8. Trademark Licence
The reseller may use the principal's trademarks solely to promote the products
in accordance with the principal's brand guidelines. All goodwill arising from
such use vests in the principal. The principal may require the reseller to
withdraw any marketing material at the reseller's expense.

9. End Customer Data
The reseller shall provide the principal with the name, contact details,
installed base, and renewal dates of every end customer within fifteen days of
each sale. The principal may contact those end customers directly for support,
marketing, and renewal purposes without involving the reseller.

10. Warranty Handling
The reseller shall provide first-line support to end customers at its own cost
and shall pass through the principal's standard twelve-month limited warranty
without extension or modification. The reseller bears all costs of collection,
inspection, and return shipping for warranty claims.

11. Indemnity by the Reseller
The reseller shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the principal and its
affiliates, officers, and employees against all claims, losses, damages, fines,
and costs including legal fees arising from the reseller's marketing statements,
any breach of this Agreement, or any act of its personnel or subcontractors.

12. Limitation of Liability
The principal's total liability arising out of or in connection with this
Agreement shall not exceed the amount paid by the reseller for the specific
product giving rise to the claim. The principal excludes all liability for lost
profit, lost sales, and loss of business opportunity.

13. Termination
The principal may terminate this Agreement for convenience on sixty days
written notice. The reseller may terminate only for the principal's material
breach uncured after ninety days notice. On termination the reseller shall cease
all use of the trademarks and may not solicit any end customer for two years.

14. Stock on Termination
On termination the reseller may sell remaining stock for ninety days, after
which the principal may repurchase unsold stock at seventy percent of the price
paid, less any amounts owed. The reseller bears the cost of returning stock to
the principal's nominated warehouse.

15. Governing Law and Arbitration
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Dubai International Financial
Centre. Disputes shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in Dubai under
the DIFC-LCIA rules, conducted in English by a single arbitrator, with each
party bearing its own costs regardless of outcome.
