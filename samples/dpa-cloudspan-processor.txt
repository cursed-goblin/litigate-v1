DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the Cloud Services Agreement dated
4 January 2026 between Meridian Systems Private Limited, the controller, and
Cloudspan Infrastructure Inc, a Delaware corporation, the processor. This
Addendum governs the processing of personal data by the processor on behalf of
the controller in the course of providing hosting and analytics services.

1. Scope and Roles
The controller determines the purposes and means of processing personal data and
the processor acts only on the controller's documented instructions. The
processor shall notify the controller without undue delay if it believes an
instruction infringes applicable data protection law, and may suspend the
relevant processing until the instruction is confirmed or amended.

2. Nature of Processing
The processor will process customer account records, contact details, usage
telemetry, support correspondence, and billing information for the purpose of
delivering, securing, monitoring, and improving the services described in the
Cloud Services Agreement, and for no other purpose whatsoever.

3. Subprocessors
The controller gives general authorisation for the processor to engage
subprocessors. The processor will publish a list of subprocessors and will give
ten days notice by email before adding a new one. If the controller objects, its
only remedy is to terminate the affected service on thirty days notice without
refund of prepaid fees.

4. International Transfers
Personal data may be transferred to and processed in the United States,
Ireland, and Singapore. Where such transfer is from a jurisdiction requiring a
transfer mechanism, the parties agree that the applicable standard contractual
clauses are incorporated into this Addendum by reference and take precedence in
the event of conflict.

5. Security Measures
The processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures
including encryption of personal data in transit and at rest, role-based access
control, logging of administrative access, annual penetration testing, and
background checks on personnel with access to production systems.

6. Personnel and Confidentiality
The processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process personal data are
subject to binding confidentiality obligations that survive the end of their
engagement, and shall limit access to those personnel who require it to perform
the services. Access rights shall be reviewed at least once every quarter.

7. Breach Notification
The processor shall notify the controller of a personal data breach affecting
the controller's data within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of it, and
shall provide the information reasonably required for the controller to meet its
own regulatory notification duties, including the categories and approximate
number of records affected.

8. Assistance and Data Subject Requests
The processor shall provide reasonable assistance with data subject access,
correction, deletion, and portability requests. The processor may charge its
then-current professional services rates for assistance exceeding four hours in
any calendar month, and shall provide an estimate before incurring such charges.

9. Audit Rights
The controller may audit the processor's compliance with this Addendum once per
calendar year on thirty days written notice, during business hours, at the
controller's cost. The processor may satisfy an audit request by supplying its
most recent third-party security report instead of permitting on-site access.

10. Deletion and Return
On termination of the Cloud Services Agreement the processor shall delete all
personal data within ninety days, except copies held in encrypted backups which
are deleted on their ordinary rolling schedule of up to twelve months, and
except where retention is required by applicable law.

11. Liability
Each party's liability under this Addendum is subject to the limitations and
exclusions of liability set out in the Cloud Services Agreement, and the parties
agree that any claim under this Addendum counts against the same aggregate cap
rather than creating a separate one.

12. Order of Precedence
In the event of conflict between this Addendum and the Cloud Services
Agreement, this Addendum prevails in respect of the processing of personal data.
All other terms of the Cloud Services Agreement remain in full force and effect
and are not varied by this Addendum.
